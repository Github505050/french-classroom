Design thinking

Good design makes
the complex feel easy.

The focus throughout the project: content that is easy to find, a consistent visual language and layouts that remain comfortable on a phone.

01 / CLARITY

Easy-to-follow pages

Clearly grouped learning activities, readable cards and familiar navigation help users focus on the lesson.

02 / RESPONSIVENESS

Designed for small screens

Flexible layouts, adaptive columns and comfortably sized controls support both desktop and mobile use.

03 / CONSISTENCY

One connected experience

Shared type, spacing and colours create a cohesive interface across lessons, progress views and reporting.
